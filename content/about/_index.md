      title: What is KGI4NFDI?
      subtitle: Advancing Open Research Through Knowledge Graphs.
      text: |
        The **Knowledge Graph Infrastructure for the German National Research Data Infrastructure (KGI4NFDI)** is basic service of the [Base4NFDI program](https://base4nfdi.de/) to support the [NFDI](https://www.nfdi.de/?lang=en). It facilitates the creation, exploration, and integration of **Knowledge Graphs (KGs)**, enabling interdisciplinary collaboration and fostering open, FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable) research.

        Designed for researchers, consortia, and institutions, KGI4NFDI is an open platform dedicated to making data more interoperable and accessible. By leveraging community-driven practices, it supports transparent and sustainable advancements in research data management.

  - block: markdown

      title: Our Mission
      subtitle: Collaboration for Open Science.
      text: |
        KGI4NFDI seeks to:

        - **Support Interdisciplinary Research**: Build tools and resources that connect data across disciplines to enable new discoveries.
        - **Simplify Knowledge Graph Use**: Provide clear guidelines, training, and open tools to help researchers effectively create and use knowledge graphs.
        - **Advance Open Science**: Promote openness by adhering to FAIR principles and using open standards and technologies to make data reusable across diverse contexts.

        We believe in the power of collaboration to create an inclusive ecosystem where knowledge is openly shared and discoveries are accelerated.

  - block: markdown
    content:
      title: How It Works
      subtitle: Open Tools for Research Communities.
      text: |
        KGI4NFDI focuses on:

        - **Service Hub**: A central platform for accessing, searching, and querying knowledge graphs through an open registry and interactive tools.
        - **Community Support**: Tailored consulting, comprehensive documentation, and shared best practices for knowledge graph development and use.
        - **Interoperability**: Strategies to harmonize data standards, ensuring compatibility across disciplines and integration with global initiatives like the European Open Science Cloud (EOSC).

        All resources are freely accessible, designed to lower barriers to participation, and built with input from the research community.

        KGI4NFDI is powered by a consortium of research organizations and experts committed to advancing open science and data interoperability. Key contributors include:

        - **ZB MED – Information Centre for Life Sciences**
        - **TIB – Leibniz Information Centre for Science and Technology**
        - **GESIS – Leibniz Institute for the Social Sciences**
        - **Mannheim University Library**
        - **FIZ Karlsruhe – Leibniz Institute for Information Infrastructure**
        - **ZBW – Leibniz Information Centre for Economics**

        These institutions collaborate with researchers, data stewards, and technologists to ensure the platform reflects the needs and values of the research community.
